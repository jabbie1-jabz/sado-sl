What is it? 
Free static website hosting with drag-and-drop deployment

How to deploy:
1. Visit: https://app.netlify.com/drop

4. Wait 30 seconds

✅ Free forever

Cons:

OPTION 2: GITHUB PAGES (FREE FOREVER)
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

What is it?
GitHub's free website hosting service

How to deploy:
1. Create account: https://github.com/signup
2. Create new repository named: username.github.io
3. Upload your files (customer.html, admin.html, logo.jpg)
4. Enable GitHub Pages
5. Done!

Example result:
https://yourusername.github.io/customer.html

Time needed: 10 minutes
Cost: FREE FOREVER
Reliability: Excellent

Pros:
✅ Completely free (even after 10 years)
✅ Version control (track changes)
✅ Professional for developers
✅ Easy updates
✅ Large community support

Cons:
❌ Requires GitHub account
❌ Slightly more steps than Netlify
❌ Less beginner-friendly

Best for: Long-term hosting, developers, version control

AFTER DEPLOYMENT - WHAT YOU NEED TO DO
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1. TEST YOUR SITE
   - Visit your new URL on computer & phone
   - Click all links
   - Try WhatsApp integration
   - Test inquiry form

2. ACCESS ADMIN
   - Go to customer page
   - Press: Ctrl + Shift + A
   - Click 🔐 button
   - Login with password: admin123

3. UPLOAD YOUR FIRST PRODUCT
   - From admin, click "Add Product"
   - Fill in details
   - Upload an image
   - Click "Publish"
   - Go back to customer page
   - Your product appears!

4. CHANGE YOUR PASSWORD
   - Edit admin.html file
   - Find: const ADMIN_PASSWORD = "admin123"
   - Change to something strong
   - Redeploy

5. SHARE WITH CUSTOMERS
   - Share your public URL
   - They can browse products
   - Contact you via WhatsApp/Call/Email
   - Submit inquiries
