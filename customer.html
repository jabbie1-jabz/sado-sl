Our Foundation & Story

Sierra Integrated Pest Control Agricultural Development Organization (SADO) was founded on January 15, 2017, by Mohamed Lamin Jabbie with a dedicated mission: to turn agriculture and pest control into sustainable employment for Sierra Leonean youth and ensure national food self-sufficiency.

Guided by the belief that "development starts from the abdomen," SADO began by cultivating over 50 acres of land in Falaba District—producing pineapple, mango, banana, and more—with a standing vision for nationwide expansion and youth empowerment.

🌱 Vision Statement

To realize the tremendous potential within the agriculture, pest control, and development sectors—consistently meeting the demands for food sufficiency, customer expectations, and business partner satisfaction.

🎯 Mission Statement

To manage, contribute to, and build farmers' capacity by increasing agricultural productivity and providing pest control management systems, services, and developmental solutions—setting the highest standards in agro-pesticides, reliability, safety, and cost containment across Sierra Leone.
